Why do astronauts have to face painful "fingernail delamination" after working in space?

The fact:

Spacesuit gloves are so highly pressurized that they constrict blood flow and squeeze fingertips constantly. This pressure causes many astronauts' fingernails to completely separate from the nail bed and fall off after spacewalks.

What terrifying thing happens to the physical shape of the human heart during long-duration spaceflights?

The fact:

The heart physically reshapes itself into a sphere. Without Earth's gravity, the heart doesn't have to pump as hard. Because its workload drops by roughly 10%, the muscle loses shape and rounds out, though it reverts to normal later.

The center of our Milky Way galaxy actually smells like a specific type of bar?

The fact:

It smells like rum and tastes like raspberries! In 2009, astronomers discovered that a massive dust cloud at the center of our galaxy is packed with ethyl formate—the exact chemical compound that gives raspberries flavor and rum its smell.

VANILLIN
