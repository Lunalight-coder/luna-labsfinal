Chapter I: Just A Dream

It was just a dream. Atleast thats what i though ... I was hanging off from a cliff,holding on to the hand of an unknown boy. "Theia, hold on tight. please dont leave me. Not this time!", I heard him whisper in a weak,trembling voice. I gazed down. Below me was a dark abyss which seemed hungry,ready to devour anything that fell. I tried to hold on to his hand, not because he told me to. But because of my survival instincts. But my hands couldnt defy gravity. Each second my fingers loosened, bit by bit.

And then.. I fell. The distance between me and the unknown boy kept on increasing as continued being engulfed by the darkness below me. complete darkness, and it vomitted me back to the placewhere i belong - 'REALITY' I woke up,gasping.
